## Ranked gaps

1. Distinguish deferral from permission to retry. Repeated interruptions are already observable.
2. Withdraw queued offers on completion. The ledger and speech can disagree.
3. Bind private replies to their audience. Disclosure cannot be taken back.
4. Separate availability from presence. An occupied room may offer no turn.
5. Require assent before scheduling a group. An empty slot cannot supply consent.
6. Attribute evidence to observer and location. A reported event can outlive its source.
7. Require acknowledgement of a memory gap before claiming continuity.
8. Keep promised acts live when expression changes.
9. Let investigations retire without broadcasting them.
10. Distinguish an addressed group turn from conversation among others.
