# The Life and Times of Nina

Literary experiences and a top-down symbolic cognition catalog for Nina, a persistent artificial home companion.

The literature is the source, not the output of the rules. We author plausible and surprising experiences, formalize candidate mechanisms in Metis, sample generative traces, and compare them with independent narratives and consented real observations.

## Repository map

- `narratives/`: canonical fiction, starting with Book I.
- `persona/`: character and temperament continuity.
- `counterfactuals/`: authored alternate histories, distinct from observed events.
- `catalog/`: candidate domain specifications; executable Metis packages only after validation.
- `evaluation/`: criteria, generated traces and held-out evaluation protocol.
- `methodology/`: division of labor, extraction and refinement process.
- `AGENTS.md`: instructions for contributing agents.

## Status

Literary research corpus with executable formalization candidates, not an operational deployment. Stories are fiction; their events and characters are not evidence about real people. No empirical probabilities are inferred from authored frequencies. No license has yet been selected.
