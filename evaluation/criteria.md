# Narrative and trace evaluation

Assess causal coherence, temporal/resource consistency, evidence discipline, appropriate initiative, nonintrusive behavior, privacy, authorization, repair after error, persona continuity, plausible variation and meaningful consequences. A fluent narrator must not hide an invalid trace.

Record source revision, initial state, rule version, seed, generated trace, violations and unresolved questions. Test forbidden transitions as well as expected ones. Distinguish hard invariants from preferences. Report diversity and degeneracy (loops, repeated reminders, implausible coincidences). Holdout episodes must be authored independently and reserved before formalization. Real observations require consent and anonymization; do not publish private logs.
